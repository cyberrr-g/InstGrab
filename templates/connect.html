How to get the cookie (one time)

  1. On a computer, open instagram.com and log in.
  2. Press F12 (or right-click → Inspect) and open Application → Cookies → https://www.instagram.com.
  3. Copy the value of the sessionid cookie.
  4. Paste it above and press Save session.

Tip: use a throwaway Instagram account. The session cookie works like a password and expires after a while — when stories stop working, save a fresh cookie here again. Your cookie is stored only on the server and is never shown to visitors.
